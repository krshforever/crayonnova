Sketch Lab

Paper, but electric.

A crayon engine on HTML canvas — jittered strokes, speed-sensitive wax, and speckled grain. Draw fast for thin strokes, slow for thick ones. Undo, erase, export. Touch and mouse both work.

  • Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Shift+Z redo

  • Text & stickers: set them up in the sidebar, then tap the sheet to place.
